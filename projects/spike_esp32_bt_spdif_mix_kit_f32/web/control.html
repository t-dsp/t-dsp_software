Tempo

One tempo for the MIDI song AND the drum groove — they lock together and move as you drag. Accurate lock needs a song with real tempo (an SD .mid); built-in demos are estimated.

Drums

A looping groove plays under whatever you perform live. Needs a General-MIDI engine (TSF/SF2/OPL3/OPLL) — silent on Dexed/Plaits/Rings/VA. Add grooves with tools/fetch_drums.py.

SD Patch Library

/dexed

Browse the DX7 cartridge library on the SD card (thousands of voices): folders → carts → voices. Tapping a voice loads it into the Dexed pool. Dexed builds only.

MIDI mode

ReplayGain

Loudness normalization across voices & GM programs (K-weighted). Off = raw synth output. Applies to the whole synth.

Expression — controller → sound

Volume & Brightness work on any patch. Vibrato/Tremolo need an LFO — “Force” adds one to any patch; “Respect” only sounds on instruments tagged [V]/[T].
